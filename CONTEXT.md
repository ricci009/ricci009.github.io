# Personal ASCII coding desk

Riccardo Salzmann's personal website presents his experiences through objects on an interactive 3D desk drawn with ASCII characters, with direct access to supporting professional and personal material.

## Language

**Workbench**:
The three-dimensional coding desk, rendered in ASCII characters, that provides an overview of Riccardo's work and interests.

**Desk object**:
A recognizable item associated with an experience or collection of stories, such as a circuit board or diploma.

**SMT monitor**:
The broadcast monitor on the workbench that introduces Riccardo's sports graphics work and opens its project stories.

**Experience**:
A documented part of Riccardo's education, employment, freelance work, contributions, or other activities, which can contain several project stories.

**Top bar**:
The navigation above the workbench that provides direct access to the résumé, blog, and supplemental information.

**Supplemental content**:
Supporting material such as articles, external links, and further personal or professional information available alongside the workbench.
